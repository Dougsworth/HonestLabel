# Video script (aim for about 2 minutes)

Record the Lens Studio preview in Interactive Preview. Start already stepped back about a metre so the counter and all three products are in view.

0:00 Problem (voice over the untouched counter)
"Packaging lies. Made with real fruit. Heart healthy. The truth is on the back in grams, and nobody reads it. So I built Honest Label for SPECS."

0:15 The moment (click Sunny Splash)
"I look at a drink and pinch it." Let the cubes drop and the tag and verdict appear. Pause two seconds.
"Ten teaspoons of sugar, right next to the bottle, at real size. Four percent juice. Basically soda."

0:40 Compare (click Morning Gold)
"Pinch a second product and both stay, so I can compare. Heart healthy granola, six teaspoons a bowl."

0:55 Contrast (click the water)
"And water. Zero. Nothing drops."

1:05 Clear and reset (click Sunny Splash again, then Reset)
"Pinch again to clear one, or reset everything."

1:15 Why glasses
"On a phone you'd stop, scan and read grams. Here the truth sits on the product, at the scale of your own hand, and your hands stay free to keep shopping."

1:30 How it was built (show devpost/design-brief.md, then the LEAF test results)
"I planned it with the plan-lens skill, which made me move the counter further away to fit the glasses' view. Claude Code built it through CLAD and the Lens Studio MCP, with a LEAF test for every done criterion."

1:45 Real glasses
"In the preview, pinching stands in for recognition. On real SPECS, the pinch sends a camera frame to Gemini to read the label, and the cubes drop next to the real product."

1:55 Close
"Honest Label. See what's really in it."
